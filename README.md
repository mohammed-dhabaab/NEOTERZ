## 📋Project Description
NEOTERZ is our final project after 4 years of working and studying hard and smartly toward our Bachelor's Degree in the information technology field on the software engineering track!

NEOTERZ is an AI-based system to count visitors, detect thefts, and analyze data! To help businesses to increase their profits by making good decisions and securing their business!
